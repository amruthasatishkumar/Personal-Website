Most courses about "modernizing your data" start with a clean architecture diagram and a shopping list of features. This one does not, because your estate does not look like that diagram, and pretending otherwise is how modernization projects quietly fail.

Here is what this course actually is. You have data spread across too many places: an on-prem SQL Server someone stood up years ago, a Synapse workspace, a few folders full of flat files, and a pile of Power BI reports that became the source of truth nobody planned for. We are going to take that reality and move it onto Microsoft Fabric in a way that is clean, governed, and affordable. Not perfect. Clean.

Each lesson builds on the one before it. We start by learning to read your estate honestly, then land your data, shape it, serve it, migrate the legacy pieces, and keep the whole thing from becoming a runaway bill. By the end you will not just know what Fabric is. You will know how to move a real business onto it.

Work through it in order. And wherever you can, map each lesson to your own estate as you go, because that is where this stops being a tutorial and starts being your migration plan.
